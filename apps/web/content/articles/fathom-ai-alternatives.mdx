The best Fathom alternative depends on what you need to change. Choose Anarlog for local meeting data and control over AI providers, Avoma for sales workflows, Read AI for search across connected communications, Fireflies for a shared conversation library, or Sembly for turning client meetings into deliverables.

Fathom itself is still worth keeping if unlimited recordings and standard summaries cover your needs. Its free plan does not stop summarizing after five calls, and it now offers bot-free capture to eligible users. A replacement should solve a specific problem in your workflow.

**Disclosure:** I co-founded Anarlog. This comparison uses vendor product, pricing and help documentation checked on November 16, 2026. It is not a hands-on accuracy or performance benchmark. Screenshots illustrate product workflows; plan limits and rollout details come from the linked current documentation.

## Quick Fathom AI review

[Fathom Free](https://www.fathom.ai/pricing) includes unlimited recordings and transcription, instant summaries, clips and search. Its [Free versus Premium guide](https://help.fathom.video/en/articles/5290881) says the first five calls each month get advanced summaries; the General/Enhanced template remains available afterward. Premium costs $20 per user monthly or $16 per user per month billed annually.

The [Fathom 3.0 upgrade guide](https://help.fathom.video/en/articles/11577345-how-does-bot-free-recording-work-in-fathom-3-0) describes a phased Mac rollout and a limited Windows Teams alpha. Availability depends on the account, language, platform and organization settings. Eligible users get capture choices, a live summary and a scratch pad whose notes can contribute to the final recap. It is inaccurate to describe all Fathom recording as bot-based or its desktop experience as only a recording button.

### 1. Anarlog: for local ownership and provider choice

[Anarlog](/) is an open-source AI meeting notetaker with a local canonical record. Sessions, notes and transcripts live in SQLite on your computer; recordings and attachments are separate files. Markdown is an export format, so using Anarlog does not turn the database into an Obsidian folder automatically.

You can type notes while capturing microphone and system audio, then use your notes and transcript to generate a summary. Choose the speech provider and the summary provider separately. That control over storage and processing is the stronger reason to switch from Fathom; bot-free capture alone is available from several vendors.

<Image src="anarlog/blog/library/products/anarlog/desktop/2026-08-20-meeting-summary.jpg" alt="Anarlog desktop meeting library beside a structured summary" />

*Anarlog meeting summary and local library. Product-library capture, August 2026.*

#### Key features

- A notepad for your own observations alongside meeting transcription.
- Local SQLite storage, portable exports and [open-source code](https://github.com/fastrepl/anarlog).
- Configurable transcription and Intelligence providers, including supported local options.

#### Strengths vs Fathom

Anarlog fits people who want their working meeting record on their own machine and want to select the AI that processes it. The [offline setup guide](https://docs.anarlog.so/offline) covers the local path. Download compatible models first, select local speech and local Intelligence, and test a short recording with the network disconnected.

#### Limitations

Offline support depends on your hardware, operating system and configured providers. Selecting a local summary model does not make hosted transcription local. BYOK sends the relevant input to your chosen provider, and sync, sharing and integrations have separate data paths. Review the [model compatibility guide](https://docs.anarlog.so/models-and-providers) before relying on an offline workflow.

If your main requirement is a shared video library with ready-made sales scorecards, evaluate those workflows directly against Fathom and Avoma. Local ownership is useful only if it fits how your team works.

#### Pricing

[Free](/pricing/) includes local/BYOK workflows; external providers may charge for usage. Pro is $14 per person monthly or $140 yearly and adds hosted transcription and AI, encrypted CloudSync, sharing and integrations. A paid plan or local database does not by itself establish approval for regulated meeting data.

### 2. Avoma: if you're serious about sales

[Avoma](https://www.avoma.com/) combines meeting capture with scheduling, structured notes and optional revenue tools. Its useful distinction is the breadth of that sales workflow, rather than a claim that Fathom lacks CRM or coaching features.

<Image src="anarlog/blog/library/products/avoma/meeting-notes/2026-08-27-overview.webp" alt="Avoma meeting video beside sales notes, speaker talk time and Ask Avoma" />

*Avoma's meeting notes and conversation view. Source: [Avoma](https://www.avoma.com/); product-library image collected August 2026.*

#### Key features

The base AI Meeting Assistant includes recording, real-time transcription, AI notes, follow-up emails, a scheduler and saving notes to CRM records. Conversation Intelligence and Revenue Intelligence are separate add-ons.

#### Strengths vs Fathom

Consider Avoma when scheduling and structured sales follow-through belong in the same system. Test one representative CRM update and one coaching workflow during the trial; the presence of an integration logo does not establish that it writes the fields you need.

#### Limitations

The base recorder subscription is not the complete cost of every analytics feature. Decide which people need to record and which need the add-ons; viewers and collaborators are free under Avoma's published model.

#### Pricing

[Startup](https://www.avoma.com/pricing) is $19 per recorder per month annually or $29 monthly. Conversation Intelligence and Revenue Intelligence each add $29 per seat per month annually or $35 monthly. For one seat, Startup plus Conversation Intelligence is therefore $48/month on annual billing or $64 on monthly billing. Compare the [seat rules](https://help.avoma.com/avoma-pricing-recorder-seats-free-users-add-ons) with your actual team before choosing a plan.

### 3. Read AI: for connected search

[Read AI](https://www.read.ai/) is worth considering when a meeting recap is only part of the context. Its search and assistant work with connected meetings, email and messages, which can help when a decision spans several channels.

<Image src="anarlog/blog/articles/fathom-ai-alternatives/fathom-3.webp" alt="Read AI report showing a summary, topics, action items and key questions" />

*Example Read AI meeting report. Source: [Read AI](https://www.read.ai/).*

#### Key features

Read provides meeting transcripts and summaries, connected search and integrations. Its [desktop app](https://support.read.ai/hc/en-us/articles/45911611006995-How-to-Use-Read-s-Desktop-App-for-Windows-and-Mac) can record audio directly from a Mac or Windows computer. It is not limited to a bot joining supported meeting platforms.

#### Strengths vs Fathom

Choose it when the unanswered question is often in an email or message rather than the call itself. Start with only the sources you need, then test whether search finds the relevant conversation and preserves appropriate access.

#### Limitations

Connected search expands the information available to the service. Review connection scopes and sharing settings. Also distinguish a transcript from recording playback: Read's pricing puts audio and video playback on Enterprise, above Pro.

#### Pricing

[Free](https://www.read.ai/plans-pricing) includes five meeting transcripts monthly. Pro costs $15 per user per month annually or $19.75 monthly. Enterprise, including playback, is $22.50 annually or $29.75 monthly per user. Use the plan comparison for retention and organization controls.

### 4. Fireflies: when you need conversation analytics

[Fireflies](https://fireflies.ai/) organizes captured conversations into a searchable workspace with integrations and AskFred. Its current product extends into email, messages and tasks, so evaluate the specific meeting workflow you need rather than treating the whole platform as one feature.

<Image src="anarlog/blog/library/products/fireflies/meeting-summary/2026-08-27-meeting-workspace.webp" alt="Fireflies meeting workspace with topic filters, comments and a speaker-labeled transcript" />

*Fireflies conversation workspace. Source: [Fireflies](https://fireflies.ai/); product-library image collected August 2026. The allowance meter in this example is not a current plan quote.*

#### Key features

Fireflies supports meeting bots and device-audio capture. Its [desktop guide](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/1208704416-getting-started-with-the-fireflies-desktop-app) describes bot-free Take Notes, a floating pane with live transcripts and manual notes, and access to stored meetings. Bot-free capture does not establish local-only processing.

#### Strengths vs Fathom

Consider Fireflies for a searchable conversation library connected to your team's tools. For analytics, trial the Business-tier reports against a real question, such as which objections appeared across several calls. Check Fathom Business alongside it.

#### Limitations

Free transcription, AI summaries, AI credits and storage are separate allowances. The [Free-plan guide](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/4027724828-learn-about-the-fireflies-free-plan) ties unlimited transcription to eligible auto-join meetings and explains credits for summaries and uploaded files. It describes storage per seat while the pricing page labels it per team; confirm the workspace allowance before importing a backlog.

#### Pricing

The [pricing page](https://fireflies.ai/pricing) lists Free with 400 minutes of team storage, limited summaries and 20 AI credits. Pro is $10 per seat per month annually or $18 monthly. Conversation intelligence and team analytics are on Business, at $19 annually or $29 monthly per seat. Check the export tier before assuming you can move every recording out of a free workspace.

### 5. Sembly AI: for client deliverables

Sembly is now positioned around professional services and client deliverables. That is a more useful selection criterion than treating it as a universal compliance answer. Its [meeting product](https://www.sembly.ai/product/meetings/) combines notes, transcripts, tasks and key decisions; its broader platform turns meeting context into documents and presentations.

<Image src="anarlog/blog/library/products/sembly/meeting-notes/2026-08-27-tasks-summary.webp" alt="Sembly task list with assignees, deadlines and bookmarked transcript passages" />

*Sembly's task and bookmark workflow. Source: [Sembly](https://www.sembly.ai/); product-library image collected August 2026. This illustrates meeting follow-up, not the full current Studio interface.*

#### Key features

Sembly's current plans distinguish core meeting notes from multi-meeting chat, integrations, MCP access and generated deliverables. Studio credits meter AI work separately from the meeting allowance.

#### Strengths vs Fathom

Shortlist it when the next step after a client meeting is a proposal, project document or other deliverable. Review the generated document against the transcript before sending it; extracting an action item and delivering a client-ready proposal are different jobs.

#### Limitations

Security controls vary by tier. The [plan comparison](https://www.sembly.ai/pricing/) places custom SSO, audit logs and its HIPAA offering on MAX. Confirm contractual terms, configuration and data-handling requirements with the vendor instead of assuming a compliance label applies to every subscription.

#### Pricing

The current annual schedule lists Basic at $10/month, Pro at $20/user/month and MAX at $30/user/month. MAX has a three-user minimum. The page also lists a Free tier and paid-plan trials; verify the current Free allowances and monthly billing quote before purchasing. Older $15 Professional-plan comparisons do not describe this plan lineup.

## Which is the best Fathom AI alternative?

Use the problem you wrote down at the start to choose two tools to test. For local meeting data and configurable AI, start with Anarlog. For a broader sales process, compare Avoma's required add-ons with Fathom Business. For connected search, try Read AI; for a shared conversation library and analytics, try Fireflies. For client deliverables, evaluate Sembly.

If Fathom's free recap already meets your needs, keeping it is a reasonable result. Switching only helps if the replacement fixes a concrete gap without creating a more expensive one.

### Before you move your meeting history

1. **Save a representative meeting.** Export the transcript, notes and recording you need from Fathom. Open the files independently; a copied sharing link is not a portable archive.
2. **Test one complete workflow.** Capture a call, correct a name, edit the summary, find a decision and export the result. For a local setup, test the selected providers offline too.
3. **Review who can access the result.** Check calendar auto-join, recording notices, workspace membership and default sharing in both tools. Bot-free recording still requires an appropriate notice and consent process.
4. **Check the full cost and exit path.** Include seats, billing commitment, AI usage, storage, analytics and export access. Keep your existing history until the replacement's output is usable.

For a local-first option, [download Anarlog](/download/) and try one meeting before moving your workflow.
