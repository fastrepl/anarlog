We've been evaluating data retention policies of major AI providers, and Mistral is the only one headquartered in the European Union. That's not a minor detail. It means Mistral is natively subject to GDPR, stores your data in the EU by default, and was built under stricter privacy regulation than any US-based alternative.

One thing changed since we last checked this policy: Le Chat is now called Vibe. Same product, same account, new name. Mistral also tightened its training defaults, and the result is stricter than what most people assume from a "privacy-first EU company".

Here is how the policy actually works, as of September 2026.

## What Mistral Stores by Default

![Mistral's default training status across Vibe and Studio, by plan](https://ijoptyyjrfqwaqhyxkxj.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/blog/articles/mistral-data-retention-policy/mistral-training-defaults.png)

Mistral splits data handling into two products with separate controls: Vibe, the chat and agent product formerly called Le Chat, and Mistral Studio, the developer platform and API. Opting out of one does not opt you out of the other.

For Studio and the API, Mistral retains your inputs and outputs for 30 rolling days after the request is processed. This window exists for abuse monitoring. After 30 days, the data is deleted.

Training is where it gets more specific than most providers admit. On Vibe, Free, Pro, and Team users are not opted out of training by default. You, or a Team admin acting organization-wide, have to disable it in the Admin Panel. Only Vibe Enterprise is opted out of training by default. On Studio, Free mode data can be used for training unless you opt out. Mistral's own documentation does not state that pay-as-you-go traffic is excluded by default the way Enterprise is; it gives you a separate opt-out toggle instead. Treat "paid" and "excluded from training" as two different things now.

Account metadata follows a separate schedule. Identity data such as your name is kept for 5 years after account termination. Email and phone number are retained for 1 year after account deletion. Technical data such as connection logs is kept for 1 rolling year.

## Where Your Data Is Stored

By default, all data is hosted within the European Union. Mistral [explicitly states](https://legal.mistral.ai/terms/privacy-policy) that it prioritizes EU-based infrastructure and applies GDPR-standard safeguards to any non-EU provider it uses.

A US API endpoint is available if you need it. Using it routes your data to US infrastructure instead. If EU residency matters for your use case, stick with the default endpoint.

## Is Zero Data Retention Applicable?

Zero Data Retention (ZDR) exists, but it's narrower than the name suggests. It's available only on Studio's pay-as-you-go plan, and only for stateless API endpoints like chat completions, embeddings, and OCR. It does not cover Vibe, Vibe Work, agents, batch files, or anything that has to store data to keep functioning.

ZDR also is not a toggle you flip yourself. You submit a request through the Help Center or support, Mistral reviews it, and approves or denies it based on your stated reason. Once approved, it shows up in your Admin Panel's Privacy settings.

## Training Opt-Out in Plain Terms

Here is the breakdown by plan, sourced directly from Mistral's own help center:

- **Vibe Free, Pro, Team**: opted in to training by default. Opt out individually in Settings, or organization-wide if you're a Team admin.
- **Vibe Enterprise**: opted out of training by default. An admin manages the opt-in toggle.
- **Studio Free mode**: opted in to training by default. Opt out in the Admin Panel.
- **Studio pay-as-you-go**: has its own opt-out toggle in the Admin Panel, under Privacy, then Anonymous improvement data. Mistral does not publish this tier as excluded by default, so check your organization's setting rather than assume it.

If you're on a paid plan and assumed that meant your data was automatically excluded from training, verify it. That assumption is exactly what tripped up developers when this became a Hacker News discussion in early September 2026.

## GDPR and Compliance

As an EU company, Mistral operates under GDPR natively rather than as a compliance overlay. A Data Processing Addendum is available for all business customers. GDPR rights including access, correction, deletion, and portability can be exercised by contacting Mistral's privacy team directly.

Mistral does offer a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement, but only for qualifying services and on request through its Trust Center, not automatically included on every plan. If HIPAA coverage matters for your use case, confirm it applies to your specific plan before you rely on it.

## How Mistral Compares With Other AI Providers

The structural difference between Mistral and the US-based providers is still data residency by default. With [OpenAI](/blog/chatgpt-data-retention-policy/), [Anthropic](/blog/anthropic-data-retention-policy/), or Google, you're opting into EU data residency as an enterprise feature. With Mistral, EU residency is the baseline and US routing is the opt-in.

Training defaults are a different story now. Mistral's non-Enterprise training defaults sit closer to the industry norm: free and Team-tier users are opted in until they act, the same shape OpenAI, Google, and, as of late 2025, Anthropic use for their own non-enterprise tiers.

[Anarlog](https://anarlog.so) is an open-source AI notepad for meetings that supports Mistral as one of its AI provider options. When you bring your own Mistral API key, your meeting data is handled under Mistral Studio's API policy: 30-day retention for abuse monitoring, and training only if you haven't opted out in your Admin Panel.

If you've been approved for ZDR on your Mistral API account, that protection applies to requests made through Anarlog too. Nothing is stored beyond the time needed to process the result.

And if your data governance requirements change, or a different provider gets approved by your security team, you can switch inside Anarlog without changing how your notes are stored or structured. Your files stay on your device regardless.
