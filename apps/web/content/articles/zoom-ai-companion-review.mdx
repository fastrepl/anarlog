Zoom's meeting AI can be useful when you want summaries, transcripts, and follow-up inside an existing Zoom account. Its value depends on your plan, enabled features, meeting platform, and data requirements.

This is a documentation-based review, checked October 31, 2026, rather than an accuracy benchmark or a new hands-on test. We make Anarlog. Older user-review screenshots below are historical examples, not evidence of how often a current version fails.

## What Is Zoom AI Companion? The Complete Feature Breakdown

**The name has changed.** Zoom's [release notes](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0080363) retire the AI Companion name while keeping its AI features in Zoom Workplace. Current documentation separates meeting AI, My Notes, and the ZoomMate agentic workspace. Older screenshots and search results still use AI Companion.

### What are Zoom AI Companion's Meeting Features?

Zoom's [current feature guide](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0057623) lists several distinct tools:

- **Meeting Summary:** a shareable summary and next steps, with host controls.
- **In-meeting questions:** ask about the discussion while the meeting is running.
- **Smart Recording:** highlights and chapters for cloud recordings.
- **My Notes:** personal notes enhanced by transcription, with platform and account requirements.
- **ZoomMate:** search, connected sources, agents, and workflows; advanced metered features have separate license and credit requirements.

For cross-platform capture, [My Notes](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0083849) can use system and microphone audio on supported desktop paths. It does not require the separate third-party meeting bot for every Teams or Google Meet conversation. Mobile does not support the same third-party meeting capture; browser audio support also varies.

## Is Zoom AI Companion Free?

The answer is feature-specific. The current US [ZoomMate](https://zoom.us/pricing/aic) and [My Notes pricing](https://zoom.us/pricing/my-notes) pages distinguish these options:

| Option | Published allowance or price | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Workplace Basic | 3 hosted meeting summaries/month, 3 My Notes uses/month, 20 AI queries/month | Availability in your account and the feature's own limits |
| Standalone My Notes | $10/user/month monthly, or $8.33/user/month billed annually; unlimited AI note-taking | Adds note-taking to Basic; hosted Zoom meetings retain the 40-minute limit |
| Workplace Pro | $16.99/user/month monthly, or $14.16/user/month billed annually; includes My Notes | Longer Zoom meetings, up to 30 hours; admin settings still apply |
| ZoomMate | $20/user/month monthly, or $16.67/user/month billed annually; 2,200 credits/user/month | Which agentic actions consume credits and what else you need licensed |

Prices are USD per user, excluding VAT. Annual rates are displayed as monthly equivalents and charged upfront for the year. Other Workplace editions and negotiated contracts have their own terms.

There is a documentation discrepancy: that pricing page advertises Basic meeting summaries, while the [Meeting Summary help page](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0058013) still lists paid Workplace requirements. Confirm the entitlement shown in your own account before relying on the free allowance. Neither “Basic has no AI” nor “every feature is included” is a safe general rule.

Zoom's [credit guide](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0085614) says many ordinary features, including meeting summaries and note-taking, are not currently metered. Advanced agentic work can consume credits. Do not add an old $12 Custom AI Companion price to every user's Workplace bill.

## How Does Zoom AI Companion Handle Your Data?

Start with the feature's actual inputs and sharing settings. Zoom's [data-handling guide](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0057861) describes different inputs for summaries, questions, recordings, and connected-source tools.

![How Does Zoom AI Companion Handle Data](anarlog/blog/articles/zoom-ai-companion-review/zoom-alt-1.webp)

*Earlier Zoom policy excerpt using the AI Companion name. The current data-handling guide linked above governs the feature you use.*

### 1. Your Data Leaves Your Device

Meeting AI sends speech-to-text data to the relevant model. Optional screen-shared OCR and chat context can contribute to summaries. Local audio capture does not establish on-device AI processing.

### 2. Multi-Vendor Processing

Zoom uses its own models and may use third-party models; it does not say every request goes to every provider. Zoom says communications-like customer content is not used to train its or third-party models. Its third-party zero-retention arrangements have limited trust-and-safety exceptions. That commitment does not delete the summaries, transcripts, or recordings stored in your Zoom account.

### 3. Compliance Implications

The Meeting Summary help page explicitly allows customers with an active BAA, subject to availability. A university's local restriction is not a general healthcare prohibition. Verify your agreement, enabled features, recipients, retention, and organizational policy before processing regulated information. Product availability alone does not establish compliance.
