Most people land on Read AI for meeting notes and then pay for email summaries, Slack search, coaching metrics, and Ada, the Digital Twin. The bot is the other reason they start looking around. Read still joins Zoom, Teams, and Meet as a visible assistant on the calendar path, and it has a habit of spreading through an org via OAuth.

A security architect at Crayon called it *"a case study in viral Shadow IT"* in [a January 2026 write-up](https://agderinthe.cloud/2026/01/19/read-ai-the-meeting-assistant-you-didnt-invite/) after it propagated through a client's Microsoft 365 tenant. IT teams on [Microsoft Q&A](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/answers/questions/4419430/read-ai) have been asking how to block it. Read also ships Windows and Mac apps that record from the computer, so the bot is no longer the only capture path. Calendar-connected Zoom and Teams joins still use a visible assistant by default.

I still split this the same way. The first four tools below are bot-free meeting notetakers. Microsoft 365 Copilot and Slack AI cover the broader bundle: email, messaging, and search. Nobody replaces the full stack in one product. Most people do not need the full stack.

## How much does Read AI cost?

![Read AI annual pricing cards for Free, Pro, Enterprise, and Enterprise+](https://static.anarlog.so/blog/library/products/read-ai/pricing/2026-10-02-annual-plans.png "char-editor-width=80")

*Read AI's current annual pricing on [read.ai/pricing](https://www.read.ai/pricing), captured October 2, 2026. Monthly billing is 25% higher.*

Read sells per user, not per workspace. [The live pricing page](https://www.read.ai/pricing) lists four tiers. Annual billing is the default on that page.

| Plan | Annual | Monthly | Included |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Free** | $0 | $0 | 5 meeting transcripts per month, 1-hour cap, search, coaching, 16+ languages on the comparison table (the marketing cards say 20+) |
| **Pro** | $15/user/mo | $19.75/user/mo | Unlimited transcripts, 4-hour cap, 100 minutes of file uploads, premium integrations (Notion, Salesforce, HubSpot, Jira, Confluence, Zapier, webhooks) |
| **Enterprise** | $22.50/user/mo | $29.75/user/mo | Audio/video playback, video highlights, 200 upload minutes, premium support. Marked most popular. |
| **Enterprise+** | $29.75/user/mo | $39.75/user/mo | HIPAA, SAML/SCIM, domain capture, custom retention, 8-hour meetings, 300 upload minutes. Requires 5+ licenses. |

Paid plans include unlimited meeting reports, storage, and workspace access. All licenses in a workspace have to sit on the same plan. There is no admin-only seat. Volume discounts start at 100 annual licenses (10%), then 15% at 500 and 20% at 1,000.

Zoom One Pro, Business, and Business Plus subscribers can get Read's premium features through [Zoom Essential Apps](https://explore.zoom.us/en/zoom-apps/essential-app-bundle/) instead of paying Read directly.

## Best Read AI alternatives in 2026

### 1. Anarlog

![Current Anarlog meeting summary view with sidebar meetings and a DEFCON 1 summary](https://static.anarlog.so/blog/library/products/anarlog/desktop/2026-08-20-meeting-summary.jpg "char-editor-width=80")

[Anarlog](https://anarlog.so) captures system audio without joining your call or requesting calendar permissions. The canonical meeting record stays in local SQLite. Markdown is an export format, not the storage model.

You can take notes in the built-in notepad while it transcribes, then let the AI combine both. Or skip the manual notes and generate summaries from the transcript alone.

The AI stack is yours: Anarlog's managed cloud, your own API keys from OpenAI, Anthropic, or Deepgram, or local models through Ollama or LM Studio. That last option keeps the pipeline on your machine.

macOS is the most mature build. Windows and Linux are in beta. There is no mobile app and no built-in CRM. Free covers unlimited local transcription and BYOK. [Pro is $14/month or $140/year](https://anarlog.so/pricing) for hosted transcription, cloud AI, and encrypted CloudSync. 45+ languages.

### 2. Jamie

![Jamie workspace showing a generated meeting note and Ask AI](https://static.anarlog.so/blog/library/products/jamie/meeting-notes/2026-07-22-meeting-notes.webp "char-editor-width=80")

*Jamie's current meeting-note workspace with speaker-attributed notes and Ask AI. Source: [Jamie](https://www.meetjamie.ai/).*

[Jamie](https://www.meetjamie.ai/) is a desktop app for Mac and Windows, plus iOS, that captures audio from the device. Summaries include speaker recognition, action items, and custom templates. HubSpot and Salesforce sit on Pro. Ask Jamie searches across recorded meetings. Notes and transcripts are listed at 99+ languages.

Privacy is the European pitch: EU-hosted infrastructure, GDPR, ISO 27001, DORA. SOC 2 Type II is listed as pending. Audio is deleted after transcription. Customer data is not used for model training.

Jamie is closed-source and stores data on Jamie's servers. Those servers are in the EU, but they are still Jamie's.

[Current pricing](https://www.meetjamie.ai/pricing): Free is 10 AI notes per month with a 30-minute cap. Plus is €21/month billed annually (20 notes, 2-hour cap, Notion/Google Docs/OneNote). Pro is €39/month billed annually for unlimited notes, a 5-hour cap, and CRM integrations. Team is €33/user/month billed annually. There is no live transcript during the call. Summaries arrive after.

### 3. Tactiq

![Tactiq Sidebar in a Zoom meeting showing four transcript-capture steps](https://static.anarlog.so/blog/library/products/tactiq/zoom/2026-08-30-sidebar.png "char-editor-width=80")

*Tactiq's Zoom sidebar in an empty meeting, walking through live transcription setup. Source: [Tactiq](https://tactiq.io/).*

[Tactiq](https://tactiq.io/) is a Chrome extension. You join a browser call, and it transcribes in real time. No bot joins the meeting. You can highlight and tag moments while people are still talking.

The workflow automations are the reason to pick it over a notepad app. Summaries and follow-up drafts can push to Slack, Notion, HubSpot, Pipedrive, and Linear. Security claims are SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, ISO 27001, and HIPAA. Tactiq says it does not record or store audio.

[Pricing](https://tactiq.io/pricing) starts free: 10 transcripts and 5 AI credits per month. Pro adds unlimited transcripts and is billed annually. Team and Business add shared credits, SSO, and retention controls.

It only works in the browser. Native Zoom or Teams desktop apps are out of reach, and there is no in-person capture.

### 4. Granola

![Granola notepad titled Team Stand-up beside a six-person video call and recording indicator](https://static.anarlog.so/blog/library/products/granola/notepad/2026-07-19-meeting-note.jpg "char-editor-width=80")

*Granola's notepad-next-to-the-call workflow. Source: [Granola](https://www.granola.ai/).*

[Granola](https://www.granola.ai/) uses the same notepad-plus-audio idea as Anarlog. You type during the call. The AI merges your notes with the transcript when it ends. The product is built to feel like a doc, not a recorder dashboard.

[Basic is free](https://www.granola.ai/pricing) with 30 days of meeting history, not a lifetime meeting cap. Business is $14/user/month and unlocks unlimited history, Attio/Notion/HubSpot/Affinity/Zapier, MCP, and API access. Enterprise is $35/user/month for SSO, org-wide training opt-out, and admin controls. Apps cover Mac, Windows, iOS, Android, and Apple Watch.

Granola is still closed-source and cloud-backed. Bot-free capture does not mean local-first storage.

### 5. Microsoft 365 Copilot

![Microsoft Teams Recap with Copilot, AI summary, transcript, and speaker timeline](https://static.anarlog.so/blog/library/products/microsoft-teams/recap/2026-07-17-recap.png "char-editor-width=80")

*Microsoft's Recap example keeps the recording, transcript, AI summary, and speaker timeline in Teams. Copilot sits in the same surface. [View the current Microsoft guide](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/teams/meetings/recap-in-microsoft-teams).*

[Microsoft 365 Copilot](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/pricing/business) is the replacement that stays inside Microsoft if your org already runs Microsoft 365. Meetings, email, chat, and files stay inside Microsoft. Teams transcription is native. That removes the third-party bot problem that drives people off Read.

Facilitator is a pre-built Copilot agent for Teams agendas, notes, and timekeeping. Copilot Chat without the paid Copilot license is a weaker, web-grounded assistant. The paid license is what adds Copilot inside Teams and the Microsoft 365 apps.

[Copilot Business](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/pricing/business) is listed at $21/user/month billed yearly, currently $18/user/month through December 31, 2026. It requires a qualifying Microsoft 365 business license and is capped at 300 users. Add the base M365 seat on top of that number.

If you are not already on Microsoft 365, this is the wrong replacement. If you are, it covers the bundle Read charges extra for, without another vendor's assistant in the participant list.

### 6. Slack AI

![Slack AI Slackbot mobile welcome screen with Ask Slackbot anything input](https://static.anarlog.so/blog/library/products/slack/slackbot/2026-10-02-mobile-welcome.jpg "char-editor-width=50")

*Slackbot's current mobile welcome screen from Slack's first-party product video. Source: [Slack](https://slack.com/blog/news/slackbot-context-aware-ai-agent-for-work).*

If you were paying Read mainly for messaging summaries or cross-channel search, check the Slack plan you already have.

Slack folded basic AI into paid plans in 2025. [Pro](https://slack.com/pricing) is $7.25/user/month billed annually ($8.75 monthly) and includes conversation summaries, AI search, and huddle notes. [Business+](https://slack.com/pricing/businessplus) is $15/user/month billed annually ($18 monthly) and adds Slackbot as a personal agent, file summaries, translations, workflow generation, and daily recaps. Enterprise search sits on Enterprise+.

Huddle transcription covers Slack calls. It will not transcribe your Zoom or Teams meetings. It also will not replace Read's engagement scoring or speaker coaching. For the search-and-recap slice Read puts on Pro at $19.75/month billed monthly, Slack may already be doing the job.

## Which Read AI alternative is right for you?

Read AI sells five things in one seat: meeting notes, email summaries, messaging summaries, cross-channel search, and coaching analytics. Most people use one of those.

If you came for meeting notes and the bot was the problem, Anarlog keeps the record local and lets you pick the model. Jamie is the EU-hosted, CRM-connected bot-free app. Tactiq is the browser extension. Granola is the notepad.

If you use the email and messaging features, Copilot is the Microsoft-native bundle and Slack AI is the messaging-native one. Check each vendor's pricing page before you buy. These numbers move.

Anarlog is free for unlimited local transcription. [Download it](https://anarlog.so/download/apple-silicon) and run it on your next call. No account required, no calendar permissions, and nothing leaves the machine unless you send it.
