To keep a Zoom transcript, first choose the record you need. Live captions help you follow the call; they are not the same as a saved transcript. Zoom's Meeting transcript can retain text without requiring a cloud recording. Cloud-recording audio transcription is a separate workflow.

We make Anarlog. This guide follows current product documentation, checked November 1, 2026; it is not a comparative accuracy test. The older Zoom screenshots below show historical interfaces.

## Method 1: Transcribing Zoom calls using Zoom's built-in transcription

| What you need | Start with | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Live text for accessibility | Automated captions | Available during the call; captions alone are not a downloadable meeting record |
| A saved meeting transcript | Meeting transcript | Host settings, participant access, and permission to save |
| A transcript alongside a cloud recording | Cloud-recording audio transcription | Eligible account, cloud recording, audio transcription enabled |
| Personal notes enhanced by a transcript | My Notes | Account entitlement, supported client, and host controls |

Zoom's [caption and transcript FAQ](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0085668) explains the distinction. The old **Full transcript** and **Save captions** settings were replaced by **Meeting transcript** controls. Do not rely on an older caption-saving tutorial to preserve a meeting record.

### What you need to get started

- **Meeting transcript:** Zoom's [enablement guide](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0085675) lists Pro, Business, or Enterprise. A host or admin must enable it; an administrator can lock settings.
- **Cloud-recording audio transcript:** Zoom lists Pro, Business, Education, or Enterprise, with cloud recording and audio transcription enabled. This requirement applies to that recording workflow.
- **Client and permissions:** Use a supported Zoom client. Being able to see text does not necessarily give a participant permission to download it.

For My Notes, summaries, and current plan boundaries, see our [Zoom AI Companion Review](/blog/zoom-ai-companion-review/). Verify the feature in your account before upgrading: Zoom's pricing and help pages do not always describe Basic entitlements consistently.

### Setting up Zoom's built-in transcription

<Image src="anarlog/blog/articles/how-to-transcribe-zoom-calls/auto-1.webp" alt="Older Zoom web portal showing the cloud recording setting" />

*Historical cloud-recording interface. It does not show the current Meeting transcript controls. Follow Zoom's current [transcript settings guide](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0085675).*

For a retained **Meeting transcript**, sign in to the Zoom web portal and open **Settings → Meeting → In Meeting (Advanced) → Meeting transcript**. Enable it if your account permits, review automatic generation and participant viewing/saving permissions, then save. If locked, ask your admin. Account-level retention rules also affect what remains available afterward.

For a **cloud-recording transcript**, use the separate cloud-recording settings and enable audio transcription. See Zoom's [cloud-recording transcription guide](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=0064927). Turning on captions alone does not configure this output.

### Enabling transcription during a meeting

<Image src="anarlog/blog/articles/how-to-transcribe-zoom-calls/auto-2.webp" alt="Older Zoom meeting toolbar with Record to the Cloud selected" />

*Historical cloud-recording toolbar. Current controls depend on the client and account.*

**Meeting transcript:** The host opens **More → Transcript** to start it. Participants can use that menu to request transcription from the host. If saving is permitted, open the Transcript panel and choose **Save transcript** to save a **TXT** file. See Zoom's [usage instructions](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0085682). Run a short meeting first and reopen the saved file.

**Cloud-recording transcript:** Start **Record to the Cloud**. After the meeting, allow time for recording and transcript processing. In the portal, open **Recordings & Transcripts → Cloud recordings**, select the meeting, and review or download its **VTT** transcript. Transcript processing can finish later than the recording; an immediate missing file is not proof that it failed.

**My Notes:** In a supported Zoom meeting, open **My Notes → Start taking notes**. Transcription starts when supported and permitted; complete the note when finished. These are personal notes, not an automatically shared meeting record. Consult the [My Notes guide](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0083849) for client, account, and host restrictions.

If you need to find those files later, use our guide to [Zoom meeting history, recordings, and chat](/blog/see-zoom-meeting-history/).

## Best practices for automatic Zoom transcription and note-taking

Regardless of which method you choose, follow these practices to get the best results:

### Audio quality optimization

- Use a quality microphone or headset
- Choose quiet environments for meetings
- Ask participants to mute when not speaking
- Speak clearly and avoid overlapping conversations

### Meeting preparation

- Test your setup before important meetings
- Inform participants about recording and transcription
- Do a quick audio test to check levels

### Post-meeting workflow

- Review AI-generated notes for accuracy and completeness
- Edit important details, names, and technical terms
- Organize notes in your preferred system
- Share relevant sections with team members

## Frequently asked questions

### 1. How to get Zoom transcript without recording?

Use **Meeting transcript** if your host's account and settings permit. Cloud-recording audio transcription requires a recording, but that is not Zoom's only transcript path. My Notes is another option with its own account and permission requirements.

Third-party tools like Anarlog can capture and transcribe Zoom audio in real-time without requiring cloud recording to Zoom's servers.

### 2. Where are Zoom transcripts stored?

Cloud-recording transcripts appear with their recordings in **Recordings & Transcripts → Cloud recordings**. Meeting transcripts and My Notes have separate access and retention controls.

A permitted in-meeting **Save transcript** creates a local TXT copy. Cloud-recording transcripts use VTT. A downloaded copy is separate from the hosted record: check who can access each and how long each is retained.

### 3. What's the best alternative to Zoom's transcription?

Consider Anarlog if local meeting storage and choosing separate transcription and summary providers fit your workflow. First test capture, output, and exports on your computer.

Otter, Fathom, and Tactiq offer other capture and workspace choices. Use the [Zoom notetaker comparison](/blog/best-ai-notetaker-for-zoom/) to check their supported clients, allowances, and processing paths.

### 4. How accurate is Zoom's native transcription?

Accuracy depends on audio, language, speaker overlap, and terminology. Compare a short authorized recording with a known reference and check names, numbers, negation, and speaker attribution. This guide does not establish a comparative accuracy ranking.

### 5. Can I transcribe Zoom calls offline?

An active Zoom call needs a network connection. A local transcription engine does not make the call itself work offline.

You can process an authorized recording locally in Anarlog on supported hardware after downloading the required models. Configure transcription and summaries independently, and test while disconnected before relying on an offline path. See the [offline setup guide](https://docs.anarlog.so/offline).

### 6. Do I need a paid Zoom account for transcription?

It depends on the feature. Zoom's cloud-recording transcript guide lists Pro, Business, Education, or Enterprise; its Meeting transcript enablement guide lists Pro, Business, or Enterprise. My Notes and summaries have separate entitlements. Check your actual account controls and current plan terms instead of treating all speech-to-text features as one paid add-on.

Anarlog does not require a paid Zoom account for device-audio capture. Its own model, platform, and plan requirements still apply, including charges from any external provider you select.
