How long Google keeps Gemini data depends on the product, account, billing status, settings, and features you use. A personal Gemini chat, an API request, and a Workspace conversation do not share one retention policy.

Policy details reviewed October 31, 2026. Start with the surface handling your data, then check its training, review, and storage rules separately.

## Quick Reference: Gemini Data Retention by Tier


| Surface | Retention to check | Training and human review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Personal Gemini app | Usually 18-month auto-delete; 72 hours with Keep Activity off or temporary chats | Settings and feedback matter; safety review can still involve humans |
| Unpaid Gemini API / AI Studio | Abuse logs and feature storage; not a single deletion timer | Content can improve products and be human reviewed; regional exceptions apply |
| Paid Gemini API | 55-day abuse logs, plus feature-specific storage | No product-improvement use of prompts/responses; authorized abuse review remains possible |
| Qualifying Workspace account | Admin controls differ between the Gemini app and Workspace side panels | No human review or generative-model training outside your domain without permission |
| Google Cloud deployment | Model, logging, caching, grounding, and stateful-feature rules | Cloud terms apply; zero retention requires specific configuration |

The sections below explain these boundaries and link to Google's current policies.

## What Gemini Stores by Default?

For personal accounts, **Keep Activity** controls saved chats and improvement use. When on, chats can improve Google's AI models. Auto-delete defaults to 18 months; alternatives include 3 months, 36 months, or no auto-delete. With activity off, future chats are not used to improve AI models unless you send feedback. Temporary chats are not used for model training. Both still have 72-hour operational retention. See the [Gemini Apps Privacy Hub](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961?hl=en).

## The Gmail Access Story

Gmail smart features, Gemini Connected Apps, and Personal Intelligence are different controls. Do not infer Gmail access from a Gemini subscription or from a setting in another Google product.

Google's [Connected Apps guide](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13695044?hl=en) explains how to connect or disconnect apps and inspect their supported actions. Availability varies by account, device, and region. Access to personal content requires permission; automatic use of public Search or Maps information is a separate capability.

On the Gemini website, open **Customize → Connectors**; if Customize is absent, look under More. Review the apps connected to the account you actually use.

## How to Control Your Data?

Open [Gemini Apps Activity](https://myactivity.google.com/product/gemini) to change Keep Activity, auto-delete, or delete saved activity. Review Connected Apps separately. Deleting Gemini activity does not automatically delete data held by other Google services.

## Human Review in Plain Terms

Turning Keep Activity off stops improvement review of future chats, subject to feedback exceptions. It does **not** eliminate human safety review, including for temporary chats. Reviewed chats can remain for up to three years, disconnected from your account; deleting activity does not delete previously reviewed copies. Feedback can include recent chat context and uploads, so review what you submit.

## How the API Is Different?

An API key alone does not establish paid-service protection. Under the [Gemini API terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms):

- **Unpaid services:** prompts and responses can improve Google products and machine-learning technologies, with human quality review. Google says not to submit confidential, sensitive, or personal information.
- **Paid API:** the request must use a Cloud project with an active billing account. Prompts and responses are not used to improve Google's products.
- **Regional exception:** in the EEA, Switzerland, and UK, the paid-services data-use terms also apply to AI Studio and unpaid API quota.
- **AI Studio:** paid data handling can apply even when Studio is free, if the account has access to an actively billed Cloud project or is a Workspace enterprise account.

Separately, Google's [abuse-monitoring policy](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/usage-policies) retains text prompts, context, and output for 55 days. Authorized employees can review flagged content. Abuse logs are not used to train models except those specifically used for policy enforcement. That window does not describe every storage feature or unpaid-service improvement use.

Check the features your integration enables. [Context caching](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/caching) includes implicit caching and, where supported, explicit caches with expiration controls. A cache setting is not a universal deletion setting for the API.

For Google Cloud, the [zero-data-retention guide](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/resources/zero-data-retention) has separate requirements for abuse logging, caching, grounding, and stored conversation state. For example, Interactions defaults to `store=true`; achieving zero retention requires `store=false`. Search and Maps grounding retain data under their own rules, and some Advanced AI features may prevent zero retention. Do not transfer a Cloud commitment to the developer API or consumer app.

## How Retention Works for Workspace and Enterprise Plans

The [Workspace privacy hub](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/generative-ai/generative-ai-in-google-workspace-privacy-hub?hl=en) applies to qualifying editions. It commits to no human review or generative-model training outside your domain without permission. Check whether Gemini is covered by your organization's Workspace agreement rather than assuming every work-email login qualifies.

Retention differs within Workspace. The Gemini app offers admin-controlled history, with 3-, 18-, or 36-month auto-delete and up to 72 hours when history is off. Workspace side-panel conversations have separate controls, ranging from 90 days to indefinite retention. Content inserted into a document or email follows that service's retention controls.

For HIPAA workloads, Google's [current supported-services list](https://docs.cloud.google.com/docs/security/compliance/hipaa) includes Gemini in Workspace and the Gemini app, excluding Gemini in Chrome. Its [implementation guide](https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/gsuite_cloud_identity_hipaa_implementation_guide.pdf) distinguishes Workspace Core Service access from Additional Product access. Verify an applicable BAA, included functionality, and configuration before handling PHI. A paid subscription or API key is not proof of coverage or compliance.

## The Broader Context

Compare equivalent services when choosing a provider: consumer apps against consumer apps, and organizational API contracts against API contracts. Our [ChatGPT](/blog/chatgpt-data-retention-policy/) and [Anthropic](/blog/anthropic-data-retention-policy/) guides cover their own settings and exceptions. A no-training promise does not, by itself, establish zero retention or exclude every form of human access.
