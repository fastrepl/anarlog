Anarlog keeps people and organizations together with your meeting notes. You can connect a CRM to look up a contact when you need more detail.

## Connect a CRM

1. Sign in to Anarlog and open **Settings → CRM**.
2. Choose **Connect** beside HubSpot, Attio, or Pipedrive, then finish authorization in your browser.
3. Return to Anarlog. Use **Test** beside the connected CRM to check that contact lookup works. If the connection needs attention, choose **Reconnect**.

Connections are managed through your Anarlog account. You can disconnect one from **Settings → CRM**.

Close connections are not yet available. Salesforce connections currently require an installed Anarlog client app; customer installation support is still being prepared.

## Fill in a contact

Open a contact and choose **Enrich** in the toolbar. Anarlog looks for a matching record in your connected CRMs and reports which one it found. It fills only empty name, email, company, job title, phone, and LinkedIn fields; details you already entered stay as they are.

If no CRM is connected, **Enrich** stays unavailable until you connect one in Settings › CRM. If no match is found, check the contact's name or email and try again.

## Multiple email addresses

A contact can hold several email addresses. Open the contact and choose **Add email** under the Email field to add another address; each additional address can be edited or removed individually. The first email stays the primary address shown and copied elsewhere.

Anarlog matches contacts by all of their email addresses — calendar attendees, session participants, and contact search all recognize every address on the contact. Merging duplicate contacts keeps both contacts' emails: the duplicate's address is preserved as an additional email.
