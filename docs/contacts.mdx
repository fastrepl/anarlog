title: "Contacts and CRM"
description: "Connect a CRM and fill missing contact details from a matching record."
---

Anarlog keeps people and organizations together with your meeting notes. You can connect a CRM to look up a contact when you need more detail.

## Connect a CRM

1. Sign in to Anarlog and open **Settings → CRM**.
2. Choose **Connect** beside HubSpot, Attio, Salesforce, Pipedrive, or Close, then finish authorization in your browser.
3. Return to Anarlog. Use **Test** beside the connected CRM to check that contact lookup works. If the connection needs attention, choose **Reconnect**.

Connections are managed through your Anarlog account. You can disconnect one from **Settings → CRM**.

## Fill in a contact

Open a contact and choose **Enrich from CRM**. Anarlog looks for a matching record in your connected CRMs and reports which one it found. It fills only empty name, email, company, job title, phone, and LinkedIn fields; details you already entered stay as they are.

If no CRM is connected, use the link beside the result to open CRM settings. If no match is found, check the contact's name or email and try again.
