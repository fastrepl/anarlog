description: "Configure domain SAML 2.0 single sign-on, enforce mandatory SSO, and apply MFA through your identity provider."
---

Enterprise workspaces support domain-based SAML 2.0 single sign-on and server-side mandatory SSO enforcement. Today, organizations enforce multi-factor authentication (MFA) through their SAML identity provider rather than standalone in-app MFA enrollment.

## SAML 2.0 single sign-on

Anarlog supports SAML 2.0 authentication with enterprise identity providers such as Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, Google Workspace, and JumpCloud.

Identity provider (IdP) SAML registration is coordinated with Fastrepl during Enterprise onboarding because self-serve SAML XML upload in Workspace Settings is forthcoming. Email [team@fastrepl.com](mailto:team@fastrepl.com) to register your IdP metadata and bind your domain.

### Claim a domain and require SSO

Workspace Owners and Admins with SSO permissions configure domain policy in the desktop app:

1. Open **Settings → Teams** and select your Enterprise workspace.
2. Scroll to **Admin → Policies**.
3. Under **Claim email domain**, enter your company domain (for example, `company.com`) and select **Verify domain**. Claiming a domain captures future account sign-ups and email updates on that domain into the workspace; pre-existing accounts with matching emails are not added retroactively.
4. Turn on **Require SSO**. This requires a claimed domain on the workspace.
5. Click **Save policies**.

### How users sign in with SSO

1. Open `https://anarlog.so/auth` or trigger sign-in from the desktop or mobile app.
2. Select **Sign in with SSO**.
3. Enter your work email address to continue to your organization's SAML identity provider.

### Server-side SSO enforcement

When **Require SSO** is enabled for a claimed domain:

- Password and email magic-link sign-in requests for that domain are rejected with `This organization requires SSO.`.
- Non-SSO OAuth tokens (Google, Microsoft, Apple, GitHub) are blocked at token issuance on the server unless the session authenticated through SAML SSO.

## Multi-factor authentication (MFA)

Native in-app TOTP authenticator or WebAuthn hardware-key enrollment inside Anarlog Account Settings is **forthcoming**. Choose one of these supported paths today:

- **Enterprise workspaces (recommended):** Turn on **Require SSO** and enforce MFA, passkeys/FIDO2 security keys, or conditional access rules inside your SAML 2.0 identity provider. For accounts on the workspace's claimed email domain(s), non-SSO sign-ins and OAuth tokens are blocked server-side so every sign-in must satisfy your IdP's MFA policy (external collaborators on unclaimed domains are not covered by this domain SSO gate).
- **Workspaces without Enterprise SSO (Free or Pro):** Sign in with Google, Microsoft, Apple, or GitHub and protect that identity account with the provider's MFA controls.

## Revoke active sessions

- Individual users can invalidate sessions across all browsers and devices from **Account → Sign out everywhere**.
- To offboard a workspace member immediately, suspend their account in your identity provider and deactivate them through [SCIM 2.0 provisioning](/enterprise/scim).
