## Contacts and teams

- Connect HubSpot, Attio, Salesforce, Pipedrive, or Close under **Settings → CRM**. From a contact, fill in missing name, email, company, job title, phone, or LinkedIn details from a matching CRM record without replacing details you entered.
- Assign a folder to a shared workspace. New notes created in that folder sync to its members; notes already in the folder stay where they are.
- Contact summaries can finish while a meeting is still updating instead of restarting with every new transcript change.

## Recording and AI

- Keep live transcription and speaker names steady when the listener reconnects. The floating recording panel now uses the same speaker labels as the transcript.
- Choose Qwen-Audio 3.1 ASR Flash Streaming with a DashScope API key.
- Get clearer differences between Crisp, Balanced, and Detailed summaries while keeping the sections requested by your template.

## Sync and desktop

- Add extra sync device slots to a Pro subscription from your account page when you need more than the included three devices.
- Find note actions in the Linux title bar. Windows and Linux window corners and controls fit their desktop environments more consistently, including when returning from a browser sign-in.
- Use the back button in the Windows title bar to leave Settings and other sidebar pages.
