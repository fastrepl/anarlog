## Dictation and AI

- Dictate into text fields in other apps with Anarlog Pro. Choose a shortcut, hold it while speaking or use hands-free mode, and see a live preview when your provider supports it.
- Connect **Wispr Flow** for transcription and dictation with your own API key, or **Venice** for summaries and chat.
- Ask for paragraphs, bullets, or a mixture in custom summary formats. Crisp, Balanced, and Detailed now control detail without overriding your requested structure.
- See Anarlog attribution on OpenRouter requests. Thanks [@ekapratama93](https://github.com/ekapratama93).

## Transcripts and recordings

- Edit transcripts as soon as recording stops. Reassign a speaker at the cursor with Enter, split selected text into its own speaker block, and delete selected blocks together.
- Merge adjacent blocks assigned to the same speaker, keep the speaker picker inside the window, and return to reading with Escape.
- Recover missing transcript sections after a connection interruption using temporary audio, with clearer status when recovery or audio cleanup cannot finish.
- Resume recording after interrupted audio-file setup and refresh cloud transcription credentials when reconnecting long recordings.
- Keep floating controls in place when expanding or resizing them, and see when transcription is reconnecting.
- Play saved recordings and audio attachments on Linux. Thanks [@jacopone](https://github.com/jacopone).

## Notes and organization

- Ask Chat to create folders and organize meetings into them. See conversation dates and message times in Chat.
- Choose which sections a Markdown automation exports, customize filenames with the meeting date and title, and include a meeting ID to distinguish similar names.
- Choose 12-hour or 24-hour meeting times in Appearance settings.
- See activity, insights, and badges together under **Settings → Insights**. Account details and billing now have separate settings pages.
- Save edits to your account profile and use a profile photo across your account and shared views.
- Give a workspace multiple owners while keeping one protected Primary owner. Primary ownership transfers still require acceptance.

## Calendar, imports, and sync

- Load all pages of Google Calendar results and show events without titles. Thanks [@igasmi](https://github.com/igasmi).
- See connection changes, import progress, and the latest import result immediately, including when no new meetings were found.
- Keep local notes and recordings when switching accounts. Choose whether to connect the library to the new account; doing so requires a compatible app version on this device afterward.
- Recover sync uploads containing large transactions and avoid recovery loops caused by colliding rows or older transcript records. Some cloud recovery cases remain under investigation; this release does not establish that every affected account has recovered.
