## Sharing and contacts

- Choose a folder's **Default access** to share new notes with people in the meeting or everyone in your workspace once their summary is ready. Existing notes keep their access, and you can change sharing on each note.
- Read a received note in a **Shared** summary tab beside your own notes for the same calendar meeting.
- Add multiple email addresses to a contact. Calendar attendees, participant matching, and contact search recognize every address; merging contacts preserves their emails.

## Summaries and chat

- Generate detailed summaries by default. Use **Make shorter** or **Make longer** in Chat to adjust a summary instead of choosing Crisp, Balanced, or Detailed in Settings.
- Use suggested prompts to recap the last five minutes, find questions to ask next, or list action items while a meeting is recording.
- Keep reading the transcript when the automatic summary starts, and scroll the note past the floating chat panel.
- See whether a summary failure came from Anarlog, OpenRouter, or the model provider.
- Show the selected template's icon on its summary tab. Thanks [@realtonypark](https://github.com/realtonypark).

## Recording

- Transcribe AssemblyAI Universal-3.5 Pro recordings once after you stop (including older Universal-3 Pro selections), so speaker labels do not restart every minute. Thanks [@georgi4444](https://github.com/georgi4444).
- Save a preferred recording computer in **Settings → Meetings**. Other computers can keep recording when it is absent; clear the preference to choose for each meeting.
- Keep capturing the other side of a call when the microphone or system audio fails. Retry the failed source without stopping the recording.
- See a persistent warning when an audio source is unavailable, with a **Retry** action to restart it without stopping the recording.
- Keep the transcript aligned with the recording when both audio sources temporarily stop.

## Sync and reminders

- Complete the first cloud sync over slow connections without timing out while a large download is still arriving.
- Wait and retry when cloud sync is busy, instead of repeatedly retrying immediately.
- Stay signed in through temporary authentication rate limits or connection failures, and wait before retrying a failed refresh.
- Choose **Join & record** or its adjacent menu from the same meeting reminder control.

## Sign-in

- Keep sign-in tied to the request you started in the app, and reject expired or replayed callbacks.
