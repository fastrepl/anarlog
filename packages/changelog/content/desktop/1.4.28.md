## Recording and transcripts

- Keep recording and saving live transcripts while an unresponsive window reloads. You can still stop recording from the floating bar.
- Recover transcripts and speaker names after a window reload or app restart. Summaries wait for transcript repair, and an existing summary is regenerated when repair adds missing words.
- Avoid duplicate recordings of the same meeting across connected devices.
- Automatically record calendar meetings only when you accepted the invitation or organized the meeting. Skip meetings where every other attendee declined.
- Starting a recording no longer pauses music or other media.
- Transcribe retained audio with ElevenLabs Scribe V2 after recording stops. The audio stays until that transcription finishes.
- Keep numbers as numerals in on-device Parakeet transcripts on Apple Silicon.
- Show participant names on live transcripts from a personal microphone, instead of numbered speakers, including when Soniox reconnects.
- Preserve transcript speaker names when you merge or delete a contact.
- Use live transcription with Inworld, Gradium, Modulate, Alebex, or your own NVIDIA Speech NIM server. Amazon Bedrock batch transcription is available through an OpenAI-compatible gateway.
- Connect a Nari Labs API key again. Saved beta model selections move to the current models. Thanks [@realtonypark](https://github.com/realtonypark).
- Connect ChatGPT on Windows when the previous connection failed because the stored credential was too large.

## Sync, sharing, and export

- Recover cloud sync when a retry includes new edits after an earlier upload succeeded without confirmation reaching the app. Keep local edits working after interrupted sync cleanup.
- Connect a new Pro device to cloud sync automatically while another device signed in to the same account is open. A device where you paused sync stays paused.
- Pin a team workspace under your contact in the sidebar. It stays pinned while you belong to that workspace.
- Co-edit a shared note with other people who can edit it. Their changes appear as they type.
- Manage sharing for notes you created, even when your workspace role does not let you manage other people's notes.
- Save a finished summary to a Google Drive folder from an automation, including as a Google Doc. Generating the summary again updates that file.
- Find local meetings within a chosen folder in chat, the CLI, and local MCP tools.
- Keep speaker labels in Markdown transcripts exported by automations, the CLI, MCP, and the Cloud API when speaker information is available.
- Export a PDF with the session title, page numbers, and a cleaner cover instead of the old generic branding.
- **Get more slots** in Sync settings opens your account page when you need another sync device.
- Check recent uploads, downloads, and errors in the Sync settings log. Synced mobile devices show a phone icon.

## Desktop

- The floating recording bar no longer crashes session start on Linux/X11.
- Use Cmd+K to jump to pages and settings as well as notes.
- Play long recordings with less memory use and faster waveform loading. The playback-speed menu stays visible instead of being clipped.
- Reduce background loading when opening long transcripts, while keeping full-transcript copying available.
- Restore missing titles on notes created from calendar meetings.
- Keep chat reasoning available across turns when using a ChatGPT subscription.
- Keep the sidebar and macOS window controls on screen when you zoom in or out.
