## Transcription and meetings

- Keep saved transcripts when post-processing returns incomplete text.
- Preserve all requested languages during mixed-language transcription instead of returning partial coverage.

- Capture remote voices in Microsoft Teams on Windows, including after the audio output device changes.

- Use Bluetooth microphones on macOS reliably by switching to the HFP/SCO profile when the mic opens.

- Connect Nari Labs for live transcription with your own API key.
- Open informational notifications without starting a recording or changing your current note.
- Keep notes and ignored-event choices attached to recurring Apple Calendar meetings when an occurrence is rescheduled, without creating duplicates. Thanks [@Diaspar4u](https://github.com/Diaspar4u).
