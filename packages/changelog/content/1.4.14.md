## Meetings and recording

- Keep recording through a brief transcription or network drop instead of
  ending the meeting, then reconnect with a short delay instead of retrying
  immediately
- Pick a speaker output in Settings → Meetings so loopback capture follows
  the device you are actually using on Windows and Linux
- Label the other person in a 1:1 transcript, and name speakers in larger
  meetings only when a unique high-confidence voiceprint match is available
- Capture visible meeting chat from Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, Slack, and
  Webex, including more Chromium and Firefox-based browsers, then search it
  in the session
- Post a recording disclosure into the meeting chat when that setting is on
- Quit Completely from the tray or Dock confirmation without waiting for a
  background flush

## Transcription and intelligence

- Choose a local whisper.cpp `.bin` model file from Settings → Transcription
  instead of relying only on bundled downloads
- Keep Mistral batch transcription speaker labels aligned with the words they
  belong to
- Load ChatGPT subscription models from the current catalog instead of a
  stale fallback list

## Sharing and teams

- Set a default for new meeting-note shares in Settings → Meetings: **Only
  me**, **People in the meeting**, or **Everyone in your workspace**
- Email teammates when you invite them to a workspace, with a link they can
  accept after signing in
- Enforce **Require SSO** for claimed company domains so Google, GitHub, and
  email sign-in cannot bypass workspace policy
- Show **Not invited** on suggested attendees instead of a long explanation,
  and keep **Invite** as the action that actually sends access

## Sync

- Rename this computer in Settings → Sync so it is recognizable on your other
  devices
- See restored notes appear while a new device is still finishing its first
  sync
- Read a clear sync-failure message that your notes stay safe on this device,
  instead of a raw backend error

## Account and contacts

- Find **Sign out** and **Sign out everywhere** on the Account tab, not under
  Developers
- Create a contact from **Enhance contact** when the participant is not already
  in your address book

## Updates

- Check for Updates and installer downloads from anarlog.so after the rename,
  instead of the old Hyprnote desktop host

## Developers

- Propose a summary or memo replacement from the CLI or MCP, then apply or
  decline it in the desktop app

## Linux

- Keep the app running when the audio device glitches instead of crashing on
  an ALSA stream error
