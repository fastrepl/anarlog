## Summaries and transcription

- Choose Claude Sonnet, Claude Opus, GPT Sol, Gemini Pro, or Gemini Flash for Pro Cloud Intelligence, or keep Auto for managed routing.
- Keep the full generated summary instead of cutting it off at a fixed length or dropping later sections. Crisp, Balanced, and Detailed still guide how much detail the model writes.
- When you configure additional spoken languages, write summaries in the language used most in the transcript. Generated titles follow the summary's language. If detection is unavailable, use your main language.
- Select an on-device transcription model automatically when its download finishes. See download progress in Settings and a completion notification. Choosing another provider or model while it downloads keeps your newer choice.
- Recognize your microphone voice on calls even when brief background voices appear, and recognize meetings joined through Aside.

## Notes and calendar

- Pin a note window above other apps with **Always on Top** in the note's overflow menu.
- Refresh a calendar note's meeting details and timeline position when its event changes.
- Hide calendar invitations you have not accepted, including tentative and declined events, from the sidebar timeline.
- Fix an editor crash when you type a bullet after an existing list.
- Keep notification actions visible together. Clicking a notification's body expands or collapses its details, and recording-device questions stay visible until you answer Yes or No.
- Recover sync after an upload retry includes newer edits, and keep local changes tracked after interrupted sync cleanup.

## Pro

- Pro includes collaboration, shared workspaces, roles, and centralized billing at $14 per person monthly or $140 yearly. Existing paid Team workspaces keep their access.
- New Pro trials last two weeks. Trials already in progress keep their original end date.

- Keep your chosen app icon after closing Anarlog. **Quit** closes the main window while Anarlog stays running; system shutdown can still exit the app.
