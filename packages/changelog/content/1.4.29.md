## Sync update required

- Update Anarlog on every synced device. Once a workspace switches to the new sync protocol, desktop 1.4.28 and earlier can no longer sync that workspace until updated. Local notes remain available.

## Summaries and transcription

- Choose Claude Sonnet, Claude Opus, GPT Sol, Gemini Pro, or Gemini Flash for Pro Cloud Intelligence, or keep Auto for managed routing.
- Remove the fixed output-length limit for generated summaries. Crisp, Balanced, and Detailed still guide how much detail the model writes.
- When you configure additional spoken languages, write summaries in the language used most in the transcript. Generated titles follow the summary's language. If detection is unavailable, use your main language.
- Select an on-device transcription model automatically when its download finishes. See download progress in Settings and a completion notification. Choosing another provider or model while it downloads keeps your newer choice.
- Recognize your microphone voice on calls even when brief background voices appear, and recognize meetings joined through Aside.
- Identify chat requests made with your own OpenRouter key as Anarlog in OpenRouter's dashboard. Thanks [@ekapratama93](https://github.com/ekapratama93).
- Transcribe with batch-only providers once after recording stops instead of repeatedly during the meeting.
- Keep AssemblyAI speaker labels on single-channel recordings, and group Parakeet Batch text by channel and audio chunk when precise word timing is unavailable.
- Keep one default summary when the same note is created or summarized on multiple devices, and show an existing summary instead of an empty placeholder.

## Notes and calendar

- Pin a note window above other apps with **Always on Top** in the note's overflow menu.
- Refresh a calendar note's meeting details and timeline position when its event changes.
- File a calendar meeting's note into a folder directly from its event. For recurring meetings, choose **Auto-add** to put future meeting notes in that folder, then change or stop the rule from the same menu.
- Hide calendar invitations you have not accepted, including tentative and declined events, from the sidebar timeline.
- Join and record from a calendar reminder, or choose **Open meeting** to join without starting a recording. Reminders without a meeting link offer **Start recording**. Thanks [@swapnilraj](https://github.com/swapnilraj).
- Optionally save screens others share in meetings to the note's Memos. Enable **Capture shared screens in Memos** in Settings; macOS requires Screen Recording permission. This feature is a prototype.
- Recognize X Calls and Kontur Talk meeting links in calendar events.
- Fix an editor crash when you type a bullet after an existing list.
- Keep notification actions visible together. Clicking a notification's body expands or collapses its details, and recording-device questions stay visible until you answer Yes or No.
- Keep a closed saved-audio recovery notification hidden when you reopen its note or restart Anarlog.
- Recover sync after an upload retry includes newer edits, and keep local changes tracked after interrupted sync cleanup.
- Preserve offline note edits when receiving newer cloud state, and keep copied-link indicators visible across devices.
- Keep PDF headings clear of the preceding text and preserve Unicode text when converting notes to Markdown.

## Chat

- Open **Ask anything** without hovering over your note. Keep your chat draft when closing and reopening the floating panel, and keep note typing visible behind it.
- Find note actions in groups for editing, organization, and sharing, with a more compact share panel.

## Pro

- Refer friends from Settings and track your invitations. When a friend joins through your link and starts a trial, get one free month added to your trial or paid subscription, for up to three friends. Each friend gets 30 days of Pro free.
- Pro includes collaboration, shared workspaces, roles, and centralized billing at $14 per person monthly or $140 yearly. Existing paid Team workspaces keep their access.
- New Pro trials last two weeks. Trials already in progress keep their original end date.
- Pro includes five synced devices, with purchased extra slots added to that allowance. Existing connected devices and purchases are preserved.
- Stop showing personal trial prompts when your workspace membership already provides access.

## macOS

- Keep your chosen app icon after closing Anarlog. **Quit** closes the main window while Anarlog stays running; system shutdown can still exit the app.
