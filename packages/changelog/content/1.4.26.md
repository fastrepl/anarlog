## Recording and transcripts

- Keep recording through brief disk stalls and recover faster if the recorder needs to restart.
- Keep audio from being automatically deleted while transcription is still processing.
- Keep speaker names steady as live transcripts update, and restore participant labels when speaker context is missing.
- Keep the instruction corner above other windows and restore the window's minimum size when expanding it.

## Storage and organization

- Keep notes and recordings in the app data folder. Existing custom storage is moved there on launch after a successful copy. Use Markdown export automations to send notes to an external folder or Obsidian vault.
- Fill in missing contact names and companies from calendar participants while preserving details you have already added.
- See clearer messages when no transcription or AI providers are available, with more consistent settings layouts and import cards.
