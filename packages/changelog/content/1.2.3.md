<banner title="Save your CloudSync recovery key" variant="warning">
CloudSync now encrypts note data before it leaves your device. Store the recovery key in a password manager; you will need it to add another device, and Anarlog cannot recover it.
</banner>

## Sharing

- Share a view-only copy of a note with specific people or anyone with the link while keeping attachments and recordings private
- Open notes shared with you from the sidebar, and update or revoke access from the original note

## CloudSync

- Pro users can turn CloudSync on or off from Settings without deleting data that is already synced

## Meetings

- See the next meeting in the menu bar and browse upcoming events without opening the main window
- Optionally open scheduled meeting links when listening starts, capture visible chat in the memo, and post a recording disclosure in supported meeting chats
- Move through transcripts with side controls that preserve automatic following

## Reliability

- Import Voice Memos audio files for transcription and automatically repair gaps after a temporary live transcription failure
- Repair macOS Keychain access from Anarlog when a provider API key cannot be saved
- Generated summaries refresh more reliably, and incomplete legacy migrations no longer prevent startup
