## Notes and summaries

- Read summaries with headings, lists, and links instead of plain text.
- Swipe a note to delete it, pull to refresh sync, and keep navigating your notes while sync is running.
- Keep memo edits saved when leaving a note or switching accounts.
- Show a clear explanation when a transcript is too short to summarize. Retry transcription or summary generation from the note when needed.
- Reuse the same default summary across devices, show summaries created on desktop, and avoid generating another summary just because you open a synced note.
- Keep transcript speaker names when a contact is deleted or merged.

## Transcription and Pro

- Configure Inworld, Gradium, Modulate, Alebex, and Amazon Bedrock as additional transcription providers with your own credentials.
- Keep saved recordings available for retry when live transcription fails.
- Pro includes collaboration and shared workspaces. New Pro trials last two weeks; existing trials keep their original end date.
- Pro includes five synced devices, plus purchased extra slots.
- Preserve offline edits when receiving newer cloud state.

## iOS

- Continue pending sync and recording uploads for the time iOS allows after moving Anarlog to the background.
